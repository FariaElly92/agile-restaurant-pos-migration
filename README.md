# 🚀 Agile Implementation: Cloud POS & Tablet Ordering System Migration

## Executive Summary
This project outlines the end-to-end Agile migration of legacy point-of-sale (POS) systems across 5 restaurant locations to an integrated cloud-based POS and tabletop ordering platform. The objective was to decrease guest wait times by 20% and improve table turnover rates by 15%.

---

## 🛠 Project Lifecycle & Artifacts

### 1. Project Charter & Scope
* **Business Case:** Legacy system crashed during peak hours, causing revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction.
* **In Scope:** Software integration, tablet hardware setup, staff training, network infrastructure upgrades across 5 locations.
* **Out of Scope:** Kitchen equipment upgrades and third-party delivery service menu redesigns.

---

### 2. Product Backlog & User Stories

| ID | As a... | I want to... | So that... | Story Points | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-01 | Waitstaff | Take orders directly on handheld tablets | Orders reach the kitchen instantly without manual entry | 5 | High |
| US-02 | Manager | View real-time daily sales dashboards | I can manage inventory and labor costs on demand | 8 | High |
| US-03 | Customer | Pay at the table using contactless payment | I can complete my meal payment without waiting for paper bills | 3 | Medium |
| US-04 | Kitchen Staff | Receive structured tickets on digital display boards | Order errors are minimized during rush hours | 5 | High |
| US-05 | Customer | Split the bill easily among multiple dining guests | Payment friction is reduced at the end of the meal | 3 | Low |

### 4. Risk Register & Mitigation Strategy

| Risk ID | Description | Impact (1-5) | Likelihood (1-5) | Severity | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Network downtime during peak hours | 5 | 2 | High | Install secondary cellular backup router (4G/5G failover). |
| R-02 | Staff resistance to new software | 3 | 4 | High | Conduct interactive training sessions & introduce site super-users. |
| R-03 | Hardware supply chain delays | 4 | 2 | Medium | Order reserve backup hardware upfront with 15% buffer margin. |

---

### 5. Sprint Burn-Down & Retrospective Findings
* **Sprint 1 Outcome:** Successfully deployed network infrastructure to Locations 1 & 2.
* **Sprint Retrospective Insights:** Staff needed more hands-on training prior to go-live; adjusted Sprint 2 timeline to include a 2-day sandbox trial prior to launch.
